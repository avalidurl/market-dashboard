Reading the Data

Price Current ETF share price in USD. ETFs track underlying assets (indices, bonds, commodities).
Change Today's price movement vs yesterday's close. Green = up, Red = down.
YTD Year-to-date return since Jan 1. Shows how the asset has performed this calendar year.
Volume Number of shares traded today. High volume = more market interest/activity.
52W Range Where current price sits between its yearly low and high. Dot near right = near 52-week high.
VIX The "fear index". Below 15 = calm markets. 15-25 = normal. Above 25 = elevated fear/volatility.
